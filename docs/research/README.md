# Research & Theoretical Foundations

This directory stores research notes, papers, mathematical proofs, and experimental hypotheses.

## Theoretical Models

### Bureau of Public Roads (BPR) Link Congestion Latency
$$t_e(V_e) = t_0(e) \cdot \left[ 1 + \alpha \cdot \left( \frac{V_e}{C_e} \right)^\beta \right]$$
where $\alpha = 0.15$ and $\beta = 4.0$.

### Quantum Q-bit Representation
$$|\psi\rangle = \cos(\theta)|0\rangle + \sin(\theta)|1\rangle$$
with probability amplitudes $|\alpha|^2 + |\beta|^2 = 1$.

### Price of Anarchy (PoA)
$$\text{PoA} = \frac{\text{Total System Travel Time (User Equilibrium)}}{\text{Total System Travel Time (System Optimum)}}$$
Measures the degradation in network efficiency caused by selfish, uncoordinated driver choices compared to quantum-inspired coordinated routing.
